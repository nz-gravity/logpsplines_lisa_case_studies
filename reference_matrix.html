Parametric reference and multivariate correction#

The physical model supplies the fast response structure. The full matrix spline supplies smooth residual freedom. These are separate from the observation operator and from the Gaussian integration of theta.

Physical and observed references#

At each epoch, the TDI-2 mixing matrix maps six independent TM and six independent OMS sources into XYZ. Light travel times come from eight Keplerian orbit epochs; all epoch filters act on shared link sources. The known, unconditioned matrix is \(S_0(t,f)=L_0L_0^\dagger\).

The physical reconstruction uses

\[S(t,f)=L_0(t,f)R(f)L_0(t,f)^\dagger.\]

For a window \(w\), let \(A_{w,0}(f)A_{w,0}(f)^\dagger=C_{w,0}(f)\) be the nominal covariance of its normalized Fourier coefficients. We compute \(C_{w,0}\) with all epoch-pair cross terms and the actual gap/taper using FFT convolutions. Its blocks agree with the independent joint Gaussian benchmark. No fitted spectrum or injection truth determines this reference.

Before any pooling, the observed coefficients are whitened as \(z_w(f)=A_{w,0}(f)^{-1}\widetilde x_w(f)\). The fitted observation model is \(z_w(f)\sim\mathrm{CN}(0,R(f))\) independently across frequencies. Equivalently,

\[C_w(f)\simeq A_{w,0}(f)R(f)A_{w,0}(f)^\dagger.\]

This is an approximation: conditioning and a frequency-dependent matrix correction need not commute. The fit omits inter-frequency dependence and pseudo-covariance even though the reference includes their effect on the nominal same-frequency complex covariance. The library’s window ENBW correction approximately tempers information; it does not make the likelihood exact or calibrate posterior widths.

The reference carries orbital time dependence; \(R(f)\) is frequency-only for this short-segment case. A time-dependent residual would require additional time-spline fields and validation. Stationary secondary noise does not in general imply a time-independent congruence residual; the shared \(R(f)\) is an additional short-segment approximation.

Full Cholesky spline and exact conditional integration#

The residual covariance is

\[R=T^{-1}DT^{-\dagger},\qquad D=\operatorname{diag}(e^{h_1},e^{h_2},e^{h_3}),\qquad T_{jl}=-\theta_{jl}\ (j>l),\quad T_{jj}=1.\]

Three \(h_j\) fields and six real/imaginary theta fields use the same twelve cubic frequency basis functions. Knots are uniformly spaced in normalized log frequency. Every reconstructed draw is positive definite; the reported point is the mean of reconstructed matrices, which preserves positive definiteness. It is not an elementwise matrix median.

The example uses the pinned library’s stationary prior unchanged: max-normalized integrated derivative penalties with a \(10^{-6}\) ridge, and each smoothing scale \(\sigma\sim\mathrm{HalfNormal}(1.28)\). The Gaussian coefficient precision is \(P/\sigma^2\). Continuous and gapped basis/penalty hashes must agree or execution fails.

Conditional on the diagonal fields and scales, the row likelihood is quadratic in its Gaussian theta coefficients. The library analytically integrates their joint Gaussian. VI approximates only the remaining marginal posterior. Each retained guide draw receives a draw from the exact conditional theta Gaussian for reconstruction. All six theta weight fields remain in the saved posterior. Exact conditional integration does not make VI exact, and it applies here to the Whittle target, not to the exact gapped benchmark.

Three independently seeded full-Gaussian guides start at the known reference \(R=I\), with initial smoothing scales 0.3. This only changes starting values; it leaves priors and likelihood unchanged. Empirical conditional variances at rank-one singleton products are zero, so they are unsuitable initial values. Native-grid reconstruction restores the endpoint multiplicities of the library’s stationary breakpoint basis; a separate test matches the library’s own reconstruction exactly on its fitting grid.

Dynamic coarse-graining#

The gapped training coefficients supply a running-mean matrix pilot. The library accepts contiguous bins subject to a relative matrix-change tolerance 0.35, maximum width 256 and pilot window 301. The pilot selects edges only. Actual likelihood statistics are sums of unsmoothed complex cross-products, including phase, with their true per-bin counts.

Native bins within 0.1 mHz of the nominal 30, 60 and 90 mHz nulls are protected, and endpoint singleton bins preserve the reconstruction domain. Both observations use the same frozen partition. No missing time samples, truth, validation realizations or inferred PSDs select its edges. “Dynamic” here means adaptive widths selected in preprocessing; the target does not change during VI.

Whitening is performed on the native grid, so the parametric response is not replaced by a single value across a wide bin. Pooling the residual model is exact under independent coefficients only if \(R\) is constant inside an aggregate. The saved run’s native and pooled negative log likelihoods differ by about 4.9 (continuous) and 5.1 (gapped) at their reported fitted curves, or less than \(9\times10^{-5}\) per native bin. This is one point check, not a posterior error bound or a fine-grid inference comparison.

Fit selection and evaluation#

Each observation uses three VI restarts and 6,000 updates per row. Select the restart by minimum mean trailing negative ELBO, within the same training target. A plateau flag compares adjacent trailing windows: the mean loss change divided by represented native-bin count must be below \(10^{-3}\). Loss traces, row checks and restart reconstruction disagreement are saved. This numerical criterion is not a posterior convergence diagnostic.

Only after all fits are frozen, generate four independent validation records. Report their marginal complex quadratic scores separately from the exact benchmark’s joint real-Gaussian scores. Truth supplies final reconstruction errors on a prespecified grid; it never chooses bins, priors, knots or restarts.

Checks and environment#

The isolated case-study environment pins LogPSplinePSD to commit 132bc478aee7bb4de87259531e4c4a24b7f18d2f, which includes adaptive counts and marginalised theta. The published paper environment is unchanged.

Tests check reference-whitening likelihood differences, adaptive pooling of complex products/counts, exact pooling for a piecewise-constant residual, restored matrix reconstruction against the library, and a model trace with theta scales but no sampled theta weights. Additional dense controls check the complete nonstationary gapped covariance and its per-frequency reference.
